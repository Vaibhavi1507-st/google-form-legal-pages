
# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 26, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Google Forms Integration ("the App") handles information when users connect their Google accounts to view their Google Forms.

## 2. Information We Access

When you authorize the App, it may access your Google Drive files using Google's read-only Drive permission. This permission is used to retrieve and display Google Forms associated with your account.

The App does not ask for or collect your Google account password.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Google APIs is used to:
- Identify and display Google Forms available in your account.
- Allow you to select a form for use in the application.

## 4. Storage and Security

OAuth credentials are used to authorize API requests. The App should handle these credentials securely and only for the functionality described above.

The App does not intentionally retain a permanent copy of your Google Forms list. Information may be processed temporarily while you use the App.

## 5. Sharing of Information

The App does not sell users' Google account data. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the App, comply with applicable law, or where you authorize such sharing.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can stop further access by revoking the App's access in your Google Account security settings. Temporary session information may be cleared when your session ends.

## 7. Google's Services

Your use of Google services remains subject to Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the App's features or data practices change.
